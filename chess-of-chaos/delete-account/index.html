Delete Your Account

Chess of Chaos — request account & data deletion, no app install required.

Fastest option: delete it in the app

If you still have Chess of Chaos installed and can sign in, the quickest path is in-app: Profile → Delete Account. This starts the same 30-day grace period described below, and you can cancel it yourself from within the app any time during those 30 days.

Request deletion from the web

If you don't have the app installed, or can't sign in, you can request deletion here instead. Email us from the address associated with your account (or include your in-game display name) so we can find your account:

Email vymoirstudio@gmail.com

We'll confirm your request and begin the same 30-day grace period as the in-app flow.

What gets deleted

Requesting deletion — in-app or here — starts a 30-day grace period, not an instant wipe. This gives you a window to change your mind.
  • Your account profile, display name, and avatar
  • Match history, stats, and streaks
  • Cosmetic inventory and entitlement records linked to your account
  • Chaos Coins and Ad Token balances

After 30 days with no restore request, this data is permanently and irreversibly removed from our systems. Locally-stored data on your device (guest progress, settings) is separate — uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes that immediately, whether or not you also request account deletion.

For anything else about your data, see the full Privacy Policy.
